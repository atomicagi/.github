# Atomic AGI

Atomic AGI is an agentic marketing platform for AI search optimization.

Bring together SEO and AI search analytics and visibility tracking, content and entity strategy, brand reputation analysis, technical audits including Agent Experience (AX), and execution through AI agents, content workflows, and MCP tools.

**Built by Omnius**, a B2B SEO & AI search optimization (AEO/GEO) agency for SaaS, Fintech and AI.
